Executive Overview

ABC Company is a subscription-based property management and intelligence platform designed to become the trusted advisor for homeowners, landlords, and property managers.

Unlike traditional home service companies that respond after equipment fails, ABC Company focuses on proactive property management through digital asset tracking, annual property health assessments, maintenance planning, and vendor coordination.

Every customer receives a comprehensive digital Property Passport™, which serves as a permanent record of the property's major systems, including HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, appliances, warranties, service history, maintenance schedules, and capital improvement recommendations. This transforms fragmented home records into a single, organized source of truth.

ABC Company operates on a recurring membership model, creating predictable revenue while building long-term customer relationships. Members receive ongoing maintenance reminders, annual Home Health Assessments, property condition scoring, warranty management, and a dedicated team that coordinates maintenance and improvement projects with trusted service providers.

As properties age, the platform proactively identifies maintenance needs and replacement opportunities before they become costly emergencies. This helps property owners protect their investments while reducing unexpected expenses and improving long-term property value.

The business is designed to scale through technology, recurring memberships, and standardized operating procedures. Over time, the platform can expand beyond homeowners to serve commercial buildings, condominium associations, real estate investors, and property management companies.

Vision

Our vision is to build the operating system for home and building ownership—a platform that gives every property owner confidence, organization, and a trusted partner for managing one of their largest financial assets.

Rather than selling repairs, One Guard sells peace of mind through proactive management, digital intelligence, and lifelong customer relationships.
